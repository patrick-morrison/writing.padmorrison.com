About this site

This is where my writing lived online. During summer 2022/2023, I wrote about dive and snorkel sites around Perth, Western Australia.

This is written from Noongar boodja, I pay my respects to Elders past and present.

Bio

Patrick Morrison (he/him) is working towards a PhD at the University of Western Australia. His research interests include past climate change, submerged landscapes archaeology, 3D photogrammetry for cultural heritage and statistics. He can be contacted at: patrick.morrison@research.uwa.edu.au, and his academic website is: padmorrison.com
